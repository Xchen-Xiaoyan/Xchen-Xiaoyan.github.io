AI 行业新闻分析与 RAG 问答系统AI Industry News Analysis & RAG Q&A System

问"AI 如何改变某个行业",得到只依据真实新闻、逐句标注来源的回答;新闻库里没有答案时,系统会明确说不知道。 Ask how AI is changing an industry and get an answer built only from real news articles, with a source on every sentence. When the archive has no answer, the system says so.

要解决的问题The problem

想了解 AI 对某个行业的影响,自己翻新闻太慢,直接问大模型又无法核实,还可能编造。我希望做一个"快、而且每句话都能追溯到原文"的工具。Reading the news yourself is slow; asking a chatbot is fast but unverifiable and sometimes invented. I wanted something fast where every claim traces back to a source.

我做了什么What I built

  • 语料:13.5 万篇 AI 新闻,经过句子级网页去噪与转载去重,剩 3.3 万篇 / 18.8 万段;先用 BERTopic、UMAP、HDBSCAN 做主题建模,分析行业影响。Corpus: 135k AI news articles, reduced to 33k articles and 188k passages by sentence-level boilerplate removal and syndication dedup. BERTopic, UMAP and HDBSCAN topic modeling mapped impact by industry.
  • 检索:BM25 与向量检索混合(RRF 融合),再用交叉编码器重排序。Retrieval: BM25 and dense vectors fused with RRF, then a cross-encoder reranker.
  • 生成:要求每句话标注来源;加入"前提校验",问题涉及语料之外的时间或事件时主动拒答;中文问题先改写成英文检索词。Generation: every sentence must cite a source; a premise check declines questions about periods or events outside the archive; Chinese questions are rewritten into English queries.
  • 部署:FastAPI + Streamlit,零成本上线;调用缓存、多模型降级和每日调用上限应对免费额度。Serving: FastAPI and Streamlit at $0, with call caching, a per-model fallback chain and a daily budget for free-tier quotas.

评测:不只看"能跑",还要知道它有多可信Evaluation: not just "it works", but how far to trust it

  • LLM 生成候选题,经规则过滤、LLM 评审和检索反证三道质检,再逐题审核,最终得到 75 题(单文档 / 跨文档 / 无法回答)。LLM-written candidates went through rule, LLM-critic and retrieval-probe filters, then per-question review, leaving 75 questions (single-doc, multi-doc and unanswerable).
  • 消融实验加配对 bootstrap 置信区间:纯向量检索显著差于 BM25(Hit@10 −8.5pp,95% CI [−15.3, −1.7]);重排序让 Hit@1 提升 7–10pp。Ablations with paired-bootstrap CIs: dense-only retrieval was significantly worse than BM25 (Hit@10 −8.5 pp, 95% CI [−15.3, −1.7]); the reranker added 7–10 pp Hit@1.
  • 用更强的模型复核本地 7B 裁判:拒答判断可靠(κ 0.87),但它严重低估了幻觉(7% vs 57%)。所以忠实度只采用强裁判的结果。Validating the local 7B judge against a stronger model showed it was reliable on abstention (κ 0.87) but badly under-counted hallucinations (7% vs 57%), so faithfulness is reported from the stronger judge only.
一个负面结果:问题分解没有带来任何提升,延迟还增加了一倍,所以没有上线。追查发现,跨文档题的"漏召回"大多是标注问题:系统其实找到了报道同一事件的另一篇文章。 A negative result: query decomposition changed nothing and doubled latency, so it was not shipped. Most multi-doc "misses" turned out to be a labeling artifact: the system had found another article on the same event.
