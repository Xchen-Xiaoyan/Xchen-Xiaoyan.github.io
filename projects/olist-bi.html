Olist 电商经营分析看板(Power BI)Olist E-commerce Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

基于巴西电商 Olist 公开的 10 万笔订单、9 张业务表,完成数据清洗、星型建模、指标体系和交互看板,回答"增长从哪里来、问题出在哪里、该对谁做什么"。 Built on Olist's public data (100k orders, 9 tables): cleaning, a star schema, a metric layer and an interactive dashboard that answer where growth came from, what is going wrong, and who to act on.

核心发现Findings

  • 增长靠拉新。按 GMV = 买家数 × 人均订单数 × 客单价 拆解,2018 年 GMV 同比 +140%,买家数 +139%,另外两项基本持平;新买家贡献约 97% 的 GMV。Growth is acquisition. Decomposing GMV = buyers × orders per buyer × AOV, 2018 grew +140% YoY with buyers +139% while the other two stayed flat. New buyers made up ~97% of GMV.
  • 配送时效决定评分。延迟超过 7 天的订单平均只有 1.70 分;黑五与 2018 年 2–3 月准时率跌到 81%–88%,评分同步走低。Delivery drives reviews. Orders over 7 days late average 1.70 stars; on-time rates fell to 81–88% around Black Friday and Feb–Mar 2018, and reviews fell with them.
  • 问题高度集中。头部 10% 的卖家贡献 66.5% 的 GMV,延迟最多的 10% 卖家造成 73.8% 的延迟订单。只针对少数卖家做治理就能见效。Problems are concentrated. The top 10% of sellers make 66.5% of GMV, and the 10% with the most delays cause 73.8% of late orders, so targeting a few sellers works.
  • 召回目标明确。RFM 分层中,"高价值待唤醒"客户只占 9.4% 的买家,却贡献 21.7% 的 GMV。A clear reactivation target. RFM shows lapsed high-value buyers are 9.4% of buyers but 21.7% of GMV.

技术实现How it's built

  • 口径与建模:用 customer_unique_id 识别真实买家(customer_id 每单一个,会让复购率变成 0);订单 / 商品双粒度事实表 + 四张维度表,并以对账断言校验 GMV。Definitions & model: buyers identified by customer_unique_id (customer_id is per order and would make repeat rate zero); order- and item-grain facts with four dimensions, and GMV reconciled by assertion.
  • 33 个 DAX 指标:时间智能、复购率、漏斗转化、TOPN 头部卖家集中度、随所选指标变化的动态标题。33 DAX measures: time intelligence, repeat rate, funnel conversion, TOPN seller concentration, and dynamic titles that follow the selected metric.
  • 报表即代码:Power BI 报表以 PBIP 文本格式由 Python 生成,可以用 git 管理和审阅。Report as code: the Power BI report is generated by Python in the text-based PBIP format, so it can be versioned and reviewed in git.
怎么确认数字是对的:SQL 和 Power BI 模型各算一遍,用 DAX 查询逐项对账。这一步抓到了两个 bug。一个是 top 是 DAX 保留字。另一个是 BLANK() = FALSE() 为真,导致未送达订单被算成"延迟",圣保罗州卖家的延迟订单数因此从 6,546 修正为 5,015。 How I know the numbers are right: SQL and the Power BI model compute everything independently, and DAX queries reconcile them line by line. This caught two bugs. One was that top is a reserved DAX word. The other was that BLANK() = FALSE() evaluates to true, which counted undelivered orders as late; São Paulo sellers' late orders went from 6,546 to 5,015 after the fix.
